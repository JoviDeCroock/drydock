# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This project is for people who want package release review to be safer, more
inspectable, and easier to operate. Contributors are expected to keep discussion
respectful, technical, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Assume good intent while still asking for clear evidence.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep security-sensitive reports private until maintainers can assess impact.
- Credit ideas, reports, and patches accurately.
- Make room for people with different levels of project context.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private contact details, credentials, vulnerability details, or
  package evidence without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption after maintainers ask for the thread to stop.
- Misrepresenting project decisions, review status, or security posture.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull
requests, discussions, chat spaces, events, and any other venue where someone is
acting as a project participant.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers through the repository's
private security or maintainer contact channel. If the report involves a
maintainer, include enough context for another maintainer or project owner to
handle it independently.

Reports should include:

- what happened;
- where it happened;
- who was involved;
- any relevant links or screenshots.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, avoid unnecessary disclosure, and take
action proportionate to the situation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close threads, warn participants, temporarily
limit participation, or ban participants from project spaces. Serious security,
privacy, or safety issues may be escalated to platform administrators.

Participants who are asked to stop harmful behavior are expected to comply
immediately.
